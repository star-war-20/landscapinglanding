FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking. We believe in transparency and want you to feel confident in our process.

How much does a new driveway cost in London?
+
Driveway costs in London typically range from $3,000–$15,000+ depending on size, material (asphalt, concrete, pavers), and site preparation needed. Block paving and resin-bound tend to be at the higher end.
Are you fully licensed and insured?
+
Yes, absolutely. L&B Builders Ltd carries $2M general liability insurance and full workers' compensation. We're happy to provide certificates of insurance before any work begins.
How long does driveway installation take?
+
Most residential driveways take 1–3 days. A small tarmac driveway can be done in a single day; a large block-paved drive with edging and drainage work typically takes 2–3 days.
Do you offer any guarantee on your work?
+
Yes. All our workmanship is backed by a comprehensive 2-year craftsmanship warranty. If anything settles, cracks, or shifts within 24 months — we come back and fix it.
What areas do you cover?
+
We're based in London and cover a 30-mile radius including all surrounding towns and neighborhoods. If you're unsure, just give us a call!
